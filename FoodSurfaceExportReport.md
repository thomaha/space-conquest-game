# Food surface export cost audit

Independent 2,500 kg export service probes use existing rocket cargo terminals, baseline RP-1 and oxygen prices and finite fuel. Each actually settles the launch service, consumes propellant and pays the service provider. This is a launch-service cost increment; the orbital food voyages are separate probes.

| Surface | Propellant kg | Fuel credits | Service fee credits | Total credits/kg cargo |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| earth | 5358.03 | 21432.11 | 24.50 | 8.58 |
| moon | 737.75 | 2951.00 | 18.66 | 1.19 |
| mars | 1762.79 | 7051.14 | 20.15 | 2.83 |

Surface export does not complete an orbital transfer or a destination landing. The existing launch model supplies its own geometry, fixed carrier mass and provider capacity assumptions. Terminal construction, workforce and capital recovery are outside this service bill. No new launch coefficients or food-specific transport behavior were introduced.
