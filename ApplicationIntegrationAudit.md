# Application integration audit

Reviewed on 17 October 2026 against the live catalog and production consumers. 188 applications: 14 available, 133 partial and 41 planned.

Available means an operational consumer uses application-defined hardware. Partial means a documented subset works. Planned means no operational consumer uses that application ID; similarly named foundation-gated mechanics do not establish application support. Descriptive affected factors are design intent rather than automatic bonuses. The technology page displays these same classifications and AI avoids planned applications.

New facility construction, recipe changes, recipe production and grid generation require catalog application discovery and prerequisites. Every material-production recipe belongs to a catalog application. Launch services and surface shipyard construction use owning application research gates. Some baseline ship equipment remains foundation-gated; its researched application controls improved variants rather than unlocking the baseline. Existing ship capabilities remain frozen. No weapon values were balanced.

Verification includes paid scientist progress, command unlock gates, material-funded construction, generation and actual disk save/load. Facility construction labor remains the existing provisional daily work allowance. Production and generation tests supply settled worker allocations to the real processors.
