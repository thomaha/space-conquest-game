# Local food and offworld delivery comparison

Each source price is a current installed farm quote with its own inputs, power, maintenance, replacement allowance, 25% corporate tax and target margin. The identical application supplies a hypothetical local destination alternative at that quote. These are conditional prices, not guaranteed farm earnings. A separate orbital route starts with 2,500 kg finite food stock at the quoted source price, empty working tanks, a charged battery and a funded private carrier. Its destination bid is the carrier's cost-covering supply quote. Actual logistics, travel, fuel purchases, services, corporate tax and reserve transfers run until it returns. No household demand is consumed during this route probe. Source food is seeded orbital inventory; ground production and surface lift are not executed here. The separate surface-export audit measures lift costs. Local farm costs are held equal across sites to isolate freight; site-specific enclosure costs remain application/site constraints.
