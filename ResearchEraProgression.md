# Research era progression

Bounded 130-day runs use generated one-system campaigns, normal daily payroll and research commands. Generation and breakthrough rolls retain ordinary randomness; this verifies progression rather than balance. A version 1 disk checkpoint is reloaded on day 60. Research does not build facilities or retrofit existing ships.

## Findings and implemented changes

Every starting era retains useful research. Advanced starts open more immediate choices while keeping the same set of unknown operational applications in this catalog. Starting discoveries and application point costs were not changed. Resolved improvement counts include accepted outcomes from discoveries and refinements and vary with ordinary random rolls.

AI selection now considers missing application discovery on existing public or corporate industry, controlled-body power deficits, empty inputs at paid factories, power-plant fuel shortages and application-defined ammunition shortages on living fleet ships. Installed generation can receive urgent paid refinement. New power choices use the authoritative generation environment query. Factory and fleet requirements resolve through their actual owners and captured hardware; descriptive affected factors are not treated as implemented bonuses.

Foundation candidates lead to usable applications through prerequisite paths. Unrelated cheap fields with no useful descendant are skipped. Urgent refinements can outrank general discoveries, while other refinements follow useful discovery opportunities. Outcome decisions retain current factory capacity: urgent performance paths must fit and ordinary improvements that would pause existing factories are discarded. War still raises combat applications. Research does not create missing funds, workers, materials or ships and existing hardware remains frozen.

All 33 focused tests passed, including the three 130-day campaign runs, disk reloads, corporate factory discovery, local brownouts, capacity-safe decisions, input and ammunition shortages, prerequisite paths and existing research and power regressions. These checks establish progression in the tested starting conditions. They do not establish long-term economic sustainability, strategic optimality or full research or weapon balance.
